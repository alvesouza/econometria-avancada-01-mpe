The Prior-Conflict Dial

One measurement x of a physical quantity θ, two physicists with the same prior mean and the same prior variance. One holds a normal prior, the other a Student-t prior. Move the observation away from the prior mean and watch the normal posterior follow the prior while the t posterior lets the data win.

The single reading. Slide 39 observes x = 850.

How noisy the instrument is: x | θ ~ N(θ, σ²). Slide 28 uses σ = 40.

Read-outs

Normal: closed form (3)–(5) of L03-03. Student-t: quadrature on a 4001-point grid over min(x, μ₀) − 10·max(σ, τ₀) to max(x, μ₀) + 10·max(σ, τ₀).

Belongs to: Classes 3+4 notes, Notas/L03/ (L03-03, the normal measurement model and the four physicists).
Model: x | θ ~ N(θ, σ²). Normal prior θ ~ N(μ₀, τ₀²): posterior N(wμ₀ + (1 − w)x, wτ₀²) with w = σ²/(σ² + τ₀²); predictive x ~ N(μ₀, σ² + τ₀²). Student-t prior θ ~ tν(μ₀, s²) with s² = τ₀²(ν − 2)/ν, so V(θ) = τ₀² for both priors (slide 45: t₅(900, 240) has variance 400). Its posterior has no closed form (slide 48) and is computed by quadrature. "Pull toward μ₀" is (x − E(θ | x))/(x − μ₀); for the normal prior it equals w at every x.
