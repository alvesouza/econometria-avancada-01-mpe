Belongs to: Class 8 notes, Notas/L08/: L08-06 (ARCH and GARCH: moments, forecasts, IGARCH, kurtosis).
Model: aₜ = σₜεₜ, σ²ₜ = α₀ + α₁a²ₜ₋₁ + β₁σ²ₜ₋₁, εₜ iid N(0,1) or standardized tν (Tsay eqs. 3.14, 3.16, 3.7). Units: σ in % per period, α₀ in %² per period (Tsay's α₀ = 0.000086 is 0.86 %²). Long-run variance σ²∞ = α₀/(1 − α₁ − β₁) when α₁ + β₁ < 1. Forecasts σ²ₕ(ℓ) = α₀ + (α₁+β₁)σ²ₕ(ℓ−1) = σ²∞ + (α₁+β₁)^(ℓ−1)(σ²ₕ(1) − σ²∞) (eq. 3.17, p. 133); IGARCH: σ²ₕ(ℓ) = σ²ₕ(1) + (ℓ − 1)α₀ (eq. 3.22, p. 141). Half-life of the gap σ²ₕ(ℓ) − σ²∞: ln 0.5/ln(α₁+β₁). Excess kurtosis of aₜ (§3.16, pp. 165–166): normal shocks Ka(g) = 6α₁²/[1 − 2α₁² − (α₁+β₁)²]; tν shocks Ka = [6 + (ν+1)Ka(g)]/[ν − 4 − Ka(g)], which exists when 1 − 2α₁²(ν−1)/(ν−4) − (α₁+β₁)² > 0; the shock's own excess kurtosis is 6/(ν − 4).
Edge cases: α₁ + β₁ within 10⁻⁹ of 1 is IGARCH (no σ∞, half-life ∞); above 1 the variance forecasts explode. α₁ = β₁ = 0 gives constant variance α₀ after one step (half-life 0). When the moment condition fails the page says the fourth moment is infinite. Presets are rounded to the slider steps (α₁, β₁, α₀ to 4 decimals, σₕ(1) to 2, ν to 2). Numbers of size 10⁶ or more print in e-notation.
