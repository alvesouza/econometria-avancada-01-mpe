The AR(2) Triangle

Two lag coefficients decide whether an AR(2) settles down, oscillates in damped cycles, or explodes. Move φ₁ and φ₂ across the stationarity triangle and watch the ACF and the h-step forecast distribution respond.

Where (φ₁, φ₂) sits

Green triangle: φ₂ < 1 − φ₁, φ₂ < 1 + φ₁, φ₂ > −1. Below the dashed parabola φ₂ = −φ₁²/4 the roots are complex. Purple dots: the four presets.

Autocorrelation function

ρ₁ = φ₁/(1 − φ₂), ρℓ = φ₁ρℓ₋₁ + φ₂ρℓ₋₂ (Tsay eq. 2.13), lags 0–60.

Belongs to: Class 8 notes, Notas/L08/: L08-01 (AR(2), AR(p), cycles, identification) and L08-02 (forecasting, MA, ARMA).
Model: yₜ = φ₁yₜ₋₁ + φ₂yₜ₋₂ + εₜ, εₜ iid N(0, σ²), mean zero as on ar2-forecast.html. Solutions x of 1 − φ₁x − φ₂x² = 0; stationary iff all |x| > 1 (Tsay p. 45), equivalently the triangle. Complex iff φ₁² + 4φ₂ < 0, with modulus √(−1/φ₂) and average cycle k = 2π / cos⁻¹[φ₁/(2√−φ₂)] (Tsay p. 42). γ₀ = (1 − φ₂)σ² / [(1 + φ₂)((1 − φ₂)² − φ₁²)] (ar-ma.pdf slide 19; ar2-forecast.html prints the braces as a product, its R code divides). Forecasts: E by the recursion E(h) = φ₁E(h−1) + φ₂E(h−2) with E(0) = yₙ, E(−1) = yₙ₋₁ (Tsay p. 56); ψ₀ = 1, ψ₁ = φ₁, ψⱼ = φ₁ψⱼ₋₁ + φ₂ψⱼ₋₂, V(h) = σ²Σⱼ₌₀ʰ⁻¹ψⱼ² (Tsay eq. 2.34, p. 71). Bands use z = 1.645.
Edge cases: |x| = 1 within 10⁻⁹ is reported as a unit root (no finite γ₀, no stationary ACF); |x| < 1 is explosive. φ₂ = 0 leaves the single solution 1/φ₁; φ₁ = φ₂ = 0 is white noise. The mean and variance of the forecasts are always defined; the fan is clipped to the plot when it leaves the frame, and the read-outs are not. Numbers of size 10⁶ or more print in e-notation.
