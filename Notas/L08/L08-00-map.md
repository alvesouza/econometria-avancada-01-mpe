# Class 8 — Univariate time series · Map of the notes

> **One claim.** **A linear time-series model is a recipe for the ψ weights, and the roots decide
> everything.** Roots outside the unit circle give a stationary ARMA whose forecasts revert to the mean
> with a bounded error variance; complex roots give stochastic cycles (10.6 quarters for US GNP). **A
> root on the unit circle makes every shock permanent**, the forecast variance grows like $\ell\sigma^2$, and
> the $t$ test of $\phi=1$ has its own Dickey–Fuller law (5% point −2.86, not −1.645). Between the two
> sit long memory ($\rho_k\sim k^{2d-1}$) and the seasonal unit roots that the airline model differences
> away. **The second half moves the same ideas to the variance**: returns are uncorrelated but their
> squares are not, so GARCH is an ARMA for $a_t^2$, with persistence $\alpha_1+\beta_1$ near 1 and fat tails
> from Gaussian shocks; asymmetric variants add leverage; **stochastic volatility gives the variance its
> own shock** and needs MCMC; and **Bayesian GARCH** turns "is $\alpha_1+\beta_1<1$?" into a posterior
> probability (0.9966 for Petrobras).

Three layers per topic:

| Layer | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **U** — intuition | permanent vs transitory shocks; volatility clusters; the news-impact curve; "two shocks per observation" in SV |
| **C** — course (examinable) | the decks', the site pages' and Tsay's results with Tsay's numbering: AR(2)/AR(p) moments, roots, cycles; PACF, AIC/BIC; forecasts and ψ-variance; MA and ARMA ACFs, invertibility; random walk, drift, trend-stationarity, ARIMA, DF/ADF; ARFIMA ACF; airline model ACF and SARIMA; ARCH/GARCH moments, likelihoods, forecasts, IGARCH; GJR/APARCH/EGARCH/GARCH-M, $t$ innovations; SV moments and the Gibbs sampler; conjugate and MCMC Bayesian GARCH |

## The one result to hold

$$\boxed{\;r_t=\mu+\sum_{j\ge0}\psi_ja_{t-j}\ \Longrightarrow\ V[e_h(\ell)]=\sigma^2\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1}\psi_j^2\ \begin{cases}\to\gamma_0 & \text{roots outside the unit circle}\\ =\ell\sigma^2 & \text{random walk, }\psi_j\equiv1\end{cases}\qquad \sigma_h^2(\ell)-\sigma_\infty^2=(\alpha_1+\beta_1)^{\ell-1}\big[\sigma_h^2(1)-\sigma_\infty^2\big]\;}$$

> **The same arithmetic runs twice: once on the level, once on the variance.** In levels the ψ weights
> decide whether forecast uncertainty is bounded (L08-02 (28) vs L08-03 (6)); in variances GARCH's ARMA
> form for $a_t^2$ makes $\alpha_1+\beta_1$ play the role of the AR root (L08-06 (21), (28)), and IGARCH is
> its unit root.

## Interactive companions

  observations $y_n$, $y_{n-1}$ and the horizon $H$, or snap to the deck's, GNP's and Tsay's parameter sets.
  Watch the point in the triangle, the **stationarity verdict with the root moduli and the cycle length**,
  the ACF and the forecast fan. Defaults reproduce the ar2-forecast page ($E$ −6.795, −7.502, −7.693,
  −7.460; sd → 7.121). Linked from [[L08-01-ar2-arp-cycles-identification]] and [[L08-02-forecasting-ma-arma]].
- [The Volatility Dial](companions/companion-garch-volatility.html). Move $\alpha_1$, $\beta_1$, $\alpha_0$, the
  1-step volatility, the innovation tails ($t_\nu$ or normal) and the horizon, or snap to Tsay's GARCH and
  IGARCH and the Petrobras fits. Watch the variance term structure, the **half-life of a volatility
  shock**, $\sigma_\infty$, the kurtosis and the $(\alpha_1,\beta_1)$ plane. Defaults reproduce Tsay's Table 3.1 to
  ±0.01 percentage points. Linked from [[L08-06-arch-garch]].

Both pages are verified by `companions/check_companions.py` at three control vectors (both ends and the
default) plus degenerate cases (unit-root edge, explosive, IGARCH, infinite fourth moment), against their
own JavaScript run under `node`.

## Errata found

- **Tsay p. 137** prints $\hat\alpha_1+\hat\beta_1=0.9772$ for $0.1216+0.8511=0.9727$ → [[L08-06-arch-garch]] §4.4.
  Tsay p. 125 prints the Intel unconditional sd as 0.1315; $\sqrt{0.0111/0.644}=0.1313$. Tsay p. 154 prints
  $E(a_t^4)=3\exp(2\mu_h^2+2\sigma_h^2)$; it is $2\mu_h$ → [[L08-08-stochastic-volatility]] §4.
- **`ar2-forecast.html`**: $\mathrm{Var}(y_t)$ printed with the braces multiplying instead of dividing (its own R
  code divides and prints 7.12069); the $h=4$ lines are labelled $E(y_{n+3}\ldots)$ → [[L08-01-ar2-arp-cycles-identification]] §3.
- **`ar-ma.pdf` slide 18** loses the inequality in "$\phi_1+\phi_2\neq1$". **`unitroot.pdf`**: the AR(3) rewrite
  repeats "$\tilde\phi_2\Delta y_{t-2}$" (should start with $\tilde\phi_1\Delta y_{t-1}$); the ACF slide prints "$(k=d)$"
  for $(k-d)$ → [[L08-03-unit-roots-dickey-fuller]] §6.1, [[L08-04-long-memory-arfima]] §4.
- **`seasonal.pdf` slide 10**: in the ARIMA(1,1,1)×(1,1,1)$_{12}$ expansion the $y_{t-12}$ coefficient should be
  $(1+\Phi)$ and the $y_{t-13}$ coefficient $-(1+\phi)(1+\Phi)$ → [[L08-05-seasonal-airline-model]] §4.1.
- **`garchmodeling.html`**: the recursion is written with the standardized $\varepsilon$ (it must be the raw
  residual); the GJR and T-GARCH formulas use an indicator, but `garchFit(leverage=TRUE)` fits the
  APARCH form, so the printed `gamma1` is APARCH's $\gamma$ (literal reading: persistence 1.078); the legend
  "GARCH t(3)" is the $\nu=7$ fit → [[L08-07-garch-family-asymmetry-tails]] §3.
- **`GARCH11-SVar1.html`**: §3 is titled "Gaussian errors" but fits `cond.dist="std"`; the SV table prints a
  "p-value" of 1.9987 → [[L08-08-stochastic-volatility]] §9. **`sp500-gaussian-student-SV-models.html`** lists
  SVtl with $\varepsilon\sim N(0,1)$ (should be $t_\nu$) → [[L08-08-stochastic-volatility]] §2.
- **Not an error, a trap:** the unit-root deck's ADF −1.61 (p 0.45) for GDP and the site page's −0.37
  (p 0.987) differ because `tseries::adf.test` always adds a trend → [[L08-03-unit-roots-dickey-fuller]] §7.
