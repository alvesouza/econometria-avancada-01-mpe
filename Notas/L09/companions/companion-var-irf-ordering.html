The Ordering Dial

A bivariate VAR(1), rt = Φ rt−1 + at, with Var(a1t) = 2, Var(a2t) = 1 and correlation ρ. The orthogonalised responses are Θh = ΦhP, with P the Cholesky factor of Σ in the chosen ordering; the variance decomposition is ωij(H) = Σh<H Θh(i,j)² / Σh<H Σk Θh(i,k)². Notes: L09-02 §3–§4, L09-03 §4.
