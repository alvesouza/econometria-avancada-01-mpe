The Factor-Volatility Dial

One common factor drives two series: yit = βi ft + εit, ft ~ N(0, ht), εit ~ N(0, ψi). Then Σt = htββ′ + Ψ and ρt = β₁β₂ht / √((β₁²ht + ψ₁)(β₂²ht + ψ₂)). In factor SV the factor log-variance λt = log ht follows an AR(1); here you move it by hand. Notes: L09-05 §2, L09-07 §5.
