Data-generating process (illustrative, declared here and not taken from MHE): Y₀ ~ N(5, σ²),  z = (Y₀ − 5)/σ;  Y₁ = Y₀ + τ + γ(Y₀ − 5);   D = 1{λz + √(1−λ²) ν > c}, ν ~ N(0,1), c = Φ⁻¹(1 − π). Then E[z|D=1] = λφ(c)/π, E[z|D=0] = −λφ(c)/(1−π), ATT = τ + γσE[z|D=1], ATU = τ + γσE[z|D=0], selection bias = σλφ(c)/[π(1−π)]. Every number is closed form; no simulation. At |λ| = 1 selection is deterministic and the group densities are truncated normals.
