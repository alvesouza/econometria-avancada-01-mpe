# Class Plan — 9 classes + final exam

Built from the professor's site (hedibert.org, "Advanced Econometrics - Professional Master in Economics - 2026"), loaded into [[Aula/COURSE]]. Classes 3+4 and 5+6 are taught as pairs. Dates are not on the site. Topic rules: `rules/01`–`08`. Exercise IDs: [[Map/exercises-index]]. Abbreviations: MHE, W = Wooldridge 6e, T = Tsay AFTS 3e, I = ISLP (2023; same chapters as ISLR 2e), G = Greenberg 1e, AME = Izbicki & Mendonça (2020), MS = Morettin & Singer (prelim. 2021), B = Bolfarine & Sandoval (2010). Pages are printed pages. "Slides" means the professor's file in `Aula/<class>/materials/`. **Code is Python**; the lecturer's examples are R and are listed as reference to port.

**Assessment (site):** 30% final exam (no coding in the exam), 60% homework (4 × 15%, groups of up to 4), 10% participation. Lecturer Hedibert Freitas Lopes; TA Henrique Wang.

Weighting: statistical learning 1–2 classes (with the bias–variance part of class 1), Bayesian 2, causal inference 2, other models 1, time series 2–3 (with the AR/VAR review in class 1). Bayesian estimation also appears in every later block (SV, hierarchical models, BVAR). Expect the Bayesian versions in homework and exam questions.
